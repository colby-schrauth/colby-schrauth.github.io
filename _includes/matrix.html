{%- comment -%} The matrix: where things sit on two axes (see STYLE.md). Usage: {% include matrix.html id="map" %}, with page.matrices.map defined in front matter. `cols` and `rows` are the two sides of each axis ("Name" or "Name | small note"). `cells` run left to right, top to bottom. A cell is a map (name, eg, act) or a placed one (name, causes). A cause is "Name | share" or "Name | share | can't"; quadrants named in `lit` are drawn in brass. Optional `views`: tabs that swap each box's eg/act, keeping the box names fixed. The first view is the general one, shown as one large tab; the rest sit underneath as examples. Each view has a `label` and `cells` (eg, act) in the same order as `cells`. Each `act` sits under a small label, "What it leads to" unless `act_label` says otherwise. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign m = page.matrices[include.id] -%} {%- assign cant_seen = false -%} {%- assign act_label = m.act_label | default: "What it leads to" -%}

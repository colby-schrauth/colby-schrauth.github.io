{%- comment -%} The grid: a total drawn as squares, so you can see which part went where (see STYLE.md). Usage: {% include grid.html id="corner" %}, with page.grids.corner defined in front matter. `plan` and `actual` are [columns, rows]. Squares inside `actual` are what you kept; the rest of the plan splits into the right strip (`cols`), the bottom strip (`rows`) and the `corner` where both fell. Each label is "Name | the math". `total` is an optional bold line under the key. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign g = page.grids[include.id] -%} {%- assign pc = g.plan[0] | minus: 1 -%}{%- assign pr = g.plan[1] | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign ac = g.actual[0] -%}{%- assign ar = g.actual[1] -%}

about

Hi, I'm Colby.

I'm a Fractional Head of Data and the owner of PRIORA. I live in New Hampshire.

Before PRIORA, I worked on the Product Operations team at Google, specializing in analytics for Google Cloud. Before that I was at Weebly (we got acquired by Square), and I've taught data analytics for General Assembly in San Francisco and for Columbia University's MS in Applied Analytics program in New York.

I grew up in Eden, New York and went to Florida Atlantic University in Boca Raton.

I use this site to organize my thoughts, share my story, and hopefully help others. Thank you for visiting!

Say hello at colby.schrauth@gmail.com, or find me on LinkedIn, GitHub and Medium.

title: "Great Design x Education x Code = Internet Sensations"
date: 2017-06-14
location: San Francisco, CA
---

It's amazing to think about the volume of information on the internet, yet there's so much opportunity for more; especially when it comes to education on complex/subtle topics. For example, [baseball pitches](https://lokeshdhakar.com/baseball-pitches-illustrated/), [coffee](https://lokeshdhakar.com/coffee-drinks-illustrated/), or [learning python](https://learnpythonthehardway.org/book/). The next time you catch yourself searching...and searching...and searching – keep searching. But, if after that final search you're still in the dark, a decision needs to be made – to create or not to create.

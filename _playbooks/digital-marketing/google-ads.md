## What this chapter covers

- **Account structure.** Campaigns, ad groups, keywords and ads, kept simple.
- **Keywords and match types.** Plus a negative keyword list from day one.
- **Writing ads.** Responsive search ads, and assets like call buttons and sitelinks.
- **Bidding and budget.** Start on clicks, move to conversions once there's enough data.
- **Weekly upkeep.** The search terms report, pausing what doesn't work, and the settings to double-check (location, networks, auto-applied recommendations).

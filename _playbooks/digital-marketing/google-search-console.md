## What this chapter covers

- **Verify the site.** Use a Domain property (via a DNS record) so it covers every subdomain and `http`/`https` version.
- **Submit a sitemap.** And confirm Google can read it.
- **Read the Performance report.** Clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position, and which ones to care about.
- **Fix indexing problems.** The Pages report, what "Crawled, currently not indexed" means, and when to request indexing.
- **A monthly check.** Five minutes: new queries, pages losing clicks, any new errors.

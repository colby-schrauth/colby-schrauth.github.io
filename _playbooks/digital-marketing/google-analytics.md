## What this chapter covers

- **Create the property.** Time zone, currency, data retention set to 14 months.
- **Connect it through Tag Manager.** Rather than pasting the code into the site.
- **Clean the data.** Filter out your own visits and unwanted referrals.
- **The reports that matter.** Traffic acquisition, landing pages and key events; ignore the rest at first.
- **Link it up.** Connect Search Console and Google Ads.

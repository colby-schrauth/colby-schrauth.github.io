## What this chapter covers

- **One page per thing you sell.** Each aimed at the search someone would type.
- **Titles and descriptions.** What shows up in Google, and how to write them.
- **Site speed and mobile.** Checking Core Web Vitals in Search Console.
- **Google Business Profile.** For anyone with a location or service area.
- **Earning links.** Local directories, partners and press.

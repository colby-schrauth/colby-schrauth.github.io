## What this chapter covers

- **What UTM tags are.** The `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` bits added to a link.
- **A naming convention.** Lowercase, hyphens, a fixed list of mediums (`email`, `social`, `cpc`, `referral`).
- **A shared link builder.** One spreadsheet everyone uses so names stay consistent.
- **Where not to use them.** Links between pages on your own site, which reset the visit's source.

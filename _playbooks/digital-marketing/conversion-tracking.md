## What this chapter covers

- **Pick your conversions.** Usually three or fewer: a form submit, a call, a purchase.
- **Build them in Tag Manager.** One trigger per conversion, sent to both Analytics and Ads.
- **Mark them as key events.** And give each a value, even an estimate.
- **Test end to end.** Submit the form yourself and watch it arrive in each tool.
- **Consent and privacy.** Cookie banners, Consent Mode, and what it means for your numbers.

## What this chapter covers

- **The five numbers.** Visits, leads, cost per lead, sales and where they came from.
- **A Looker Studio dashboard.** Pulling from Analytics, Search Console and Ads in one place.
- **A weekly review habit.** What to look at, what to ignore, and when to change something.
